### Physicist brief
- Coordinates & patch: OP-1.1 uses dimensionless Kruskal coordinates with `-UV=(r/2M-1)e^{r/2M}` and the standard Schwarzschild metric (`research_program/synthesis/op11_spherical_dual_target.md:18-23`). The BH patch has `V>0`, crosses `U=0`, retains the full `S^2`, and stops at `UV<=1-epsilon_s`, so it contains complete symmetry spheres while excluding both the singularity and the bifurcation sphere; its dual contains the corresponding `H^-` cut (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:25-60`). This is a compact, mass-co-scaled patch: it contains neither null infinity nor the full singular future and therefore supports only a family-conditioned `r=2M` trapping target, not an asymptotic event-horizon claim (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:9-14,206-216`; EGS derived-md:171-195).
- Physical meaning of the signal: For symmetry spheres, `theta_k=(2/r)k(r)`, so the sign is invariant under positive rescaling of each future null normal (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:73-91`). In Schwarzschild, the future expansions distinguish exterior, trapped region II and anti-trapped region IV; the marginal locus is `r=2M` (EGS Eq. 12, `biblioteca/derived-md/Towards black-hole horizons and geodesic focusing in causal sets.md:221-230`). Thus `h_M=r/(2M)-1` and its side label are legitimate **scoring targets** in the frozen spherical family (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:93-127`). There is, however, no order-only horizon signal yet: OP-1.1 defines outputs but no estimator, and OP-1.3 leaves the bounded witness unspecified (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:152-182`; `op13_positive_evidence_protocol.md:5-15,131-142`). The old 1+1D bimodality arose because interior futures terminate at the singularity while exterior futures terminate at the box boundary (EGS derived-md:181-195); this new patch explicitly stops before the singularity, so that mechanism cannot be invoked for it.
- Sprinkling domain: The two declared channels are correctly distinct: normalized-volume iid sampling at fixed `n`, and a PPP of known intensity `rho` observing order plus `N` (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:129-150`). With fixed dimensionless patch shape, changing `M` multiplies the metric by `a^2` and the positive four-volume measure by `a^4`; normalized fixed-`n` laws are therefore identical across the mass orbit, whereas known-`rho` Poisson counts separate masses through `rho M^4` (`op12_tv_zero_3p1.md:37-70,86-104`). This forfeits absolute-mass recovery in `fixed_n` by construction. The quantity `(Vol/n)^(1/4)` is an effective conditional resolution, not an independently observed physical discreteness scale; it should not be denoted simply `ell` without that qualification (`op12_tv_zero_3p1.md:72-84`).
- Claim boundary: The defensible Phase-1 result is: a dual-closed, spherically symmetric Schwarzschild model family; an invariant scoring locus `r=2M`; exact scale-orbit degeneracy in the fixed-`n` channel; and a valid protocol for future positive separation evidence. It is not a reconstructor, a recovered horizon, an estimator of null expansion, a generic trapped-region theorem, a patch/density convergence result, or evidence that causal-set dynamics produces Schwarzschild (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:206-216`; `op12_tv_zero_3p1.md:121-162`; `op13_positive_evidence_protocol.md:131-170`). The target concerns symmetry spheres only; it must not be promoted to the boundary of all possible nonspherical trapped surfaces or to dynamical geometry (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:125-127`).

Define either an element-wise `chi_hat_P(x)` or a separate sector character `s(g)`, and remove the ambiguity between physical `chi=0`, self-dual abstention and the already available `ABSTAIN` value (`op11_spherical_dual_target.md:184-204`).
