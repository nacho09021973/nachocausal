# Causal, dynamic and infrared separation gate

> **Status:** `OPEN / CONTRACT_ONLY / NO_EXECUTION_AUTHORIZED`
> **Date:** 2026-10-01
> **Branch:** `transfer/causal-dynamic-ir-contract`

This document freezes the boundary between three different questions. It is a
methodological gate, not a result and not a new simulation authorization.

## 1. Claim ceiling

The gate may return only one of:

- `PASS`: every obligation below is met for the declared finite family and
  dynamics;
- `FAIL`: a declared obligation is contradicted by an exact counterexample or
  by a failed certificate;
- `OPEN`: a definition, proof or independent check is still missing.

None of these states establishes a reconstructed horizon, a unique spacetime,
an intrinsic physical time, a universal dynamics, or a `3+1D` signature.

## 2. Three layers that must not be conflated

For a finite causal input \(C_N=(E_N,\prec_N,\mu_N)\), define:

1. **Causal layer** \(T_{\mathrm{caus}}(C_N)\): times, pairs, or regions
   admitted by the declared order-and-number data before a dynamics is applied.
2. **Dynamic layer** \(T_{\mathrm{dyn}}^D(C_N;s)\): the support, arrival, or
   response produced by an explicitly defined dynamics \(D\), source \(s\), and
   detector. It must be generated by a state update or response kernel, not by
   relabelling a static poset summary.
3. **IR layer** \(T_{\mathrm{IR}}^D(C_N;s)\): the output of one frozen
   coarse-graining rule and one frozen scale/limit applied to the dynamic
   observable.

The inclusions or equalities among these sets are claims to be tested, not
definitions that may be chosen after looking at data.

## 3. Frozen contract

Before execution, the contract must state:

- the law of the finite causal family and the allowed `N` regime;
- the source, state space, perturbation, detector and time parameter;
- the dynamics `D`, including whether it is one model or a comparison `D1,D2`;
- the response `R_D(C_N;s,t)`, its normalization, sign, null convention and
  temporal domain;
- the definition of arrival/support and the treatment of ties and no-response
  cases;
- the single scalar or finite summary used for IR, if any;
- the coarse-graining map, scale, order of limits, tolerances and PASS/FAIL/OPEN
  rule;
- whether any external time, embedding, coordinates or hidden ground truth are
  permitted. If permitted, the claim is not order-intrinsic.

The estimator or verifier must not read coordinates, labels, the generating
embedding or a truth-derived selector. Counts remain allowed observables when
the declared channel is Order + Number; conditioning on `N=n` removes only the
global scale carried by `N`.

## 4. Minimum gates

### G0 — definition

All objects in §3 exist as executable or formally checkable definitions. A
missing response kernel, detector, time parameter or limit is `OPEN`, not an
invitation to infer one from the desired conclusion.

### G1 — intrinsic-input and leakage audit

The observable and decision path use only the declared causal input. The audit
must include a negative test that changes the hidden embedding or labels while
preserving the declared input and checks that the observable is unchanged.

### G2 — genuine dynamics

The reported dynamic quantity changes only after applying the declared state
update or response map. Static height, level, interval volume, thinning,
finite-poset summaries or a calibrated geometric score do not pass G2 by
themselves.

### G3 — scale and IR

The scale and order of limits are frozen before evaluation. `N -> infinity`
alone is insufficient: the contract must distinguish resolution, volume and
joint limits when more than one scale varies.

### G4 — reproducibility and review

Every retained table, figure or certificate has a permanent generator or
verifier. A passing local run is not independent review; review status and
canonical status remain separate.

## 5. Comparison rule

When comparing `D1` and `D2`, freeze the same `C_N`, source, detector, time
unit, sampling law, support, normalization and IR rule. Change one declared
dynamic ingredient only. Otherwise a difference cannot be attributed to the
dynamics.

The preferred target is conditional and explicit:

\[
T_{\mathrm{IR}}^{D_1}=T_{\mathrm{IR}}^{D_2}
\subsetneq T_{\mathrm{caus}},
\]

with equality and strict inclusion certified separately. A finite numerical
coincidence is not equality, and a static separation is not transmission.

## 6. Stop and reopen rules

Stop with `OPEN` or `FAIL` when any of the following occurs:

- the response or detector is undefined;
- the observable reads an embedding, hidden truth or post-hoc selector;
- the limit or normalization is ambiguous;
- the only evidence is a static diagnostic or a finite search;
- the result requires changing the observable, threshold or family after
  inspecting data.

Reopening requires a dated addendum naming the missing object, the changed
scope, the new frozen contract and the permanent verifier. No new experiment
is authorized by this document alone.
