# Asymptotic distinguishability of two random partial orders
### A horizon-identifiability problem in causal-set theory — technical memo for external consultation

*Self-contained problem statement. ~2 pages. No software, no claims beyond what is stated. Prepared to
ask whether the core question matches a known technique or constitutes a genuine research problem.*

---

## 1. Motivation (one paragraph)

A *causal set* is a locally finite partially ordered set (poset) `(P, ≺)`, the discrete substitute for a
Lorentzian manifold in causal-set quantum gravity. A finite causal set is obtained from a fixed spacetime
by a **Poisson sprinkling**: throw points by a Poisson process of intensity `ρ` and induce the order from
the spacetime causal relation. We study 1+1D Schwarzschild in Eddington–Finkelstein coordinates. A
semiclassical estimator already localises the hidden horizon radius `r_S` from order alone, but only by
**contrasting** each black-hole sprinkling against a Minkowski sprinkling on the *same* point cloud. The
question below isolates whether that contrast is **necessary** — i.e. whether "there is a trapped region"
is an intrinsic, relabel-invariant property of a **single** random order, or only a contrastive
(two-sample) property.

## 2. Formal setup

Fix a bounded coordinate patch (a box `B`) and intensity `ρ_n → ∞` (equivalently discreteness scale
`ℓ_n = ρ_n^{-1/2} → 0`). On a Poisson sample we observe **only the order** of the induced poset, up to
isomorphism. Define two ensembles of finite causal sets:

- `H_trap` — sprinkling of a Schwarzschild interior patch (an element's causal future is *focused* and
  truncated at the curvature singularity);
- `H_no-trap` — sprinkling of a **flat** (Minkowski) patch whose future boundary has been *adversarially
  shaped* so that the marginal law of the future-volume statistic (below) is matched to `H_trap`.

Let `P_n` and `Q_n` be the induced laws on **isomorphism classes of the observed (unlabelled) suborder**
under `H_trap` and `H_no-trap` respectively. The observable σ-algebra is generated by relabel-invariant
(order-isomorphism-invariant) functionals of the order: future/past cardinalities, order-interval
cardinalities `|[x,y]| = |{z : x ≼ z ≼ y}|`, joint future overlaps `|fut(x) ∩ fut(y)|`, in/out degrees,
local order-motif counts, and multiscale combinations thereof.

## 3. The adversarial matching (why the problem is non-trivial)

For each minimal-type element define the **future volume** `V(x) = |{y : x ≽ x}|` (the up-set
cardinality). Its mean is `ρ · vol(J⁺(x) ∩ B ∩ {valid region})`. **Three distinct mechanisms reduce the
same `V(x)`:** (S) singularity/horizon focusing-and-truncation; (B) the artificial future boundary of the
box; (F) generic finiteness of any finite poset near its maximal antichain. Because these enter one scalar,
`V` alone cannot attribute the deficit — and by construction (§2) `H_no-trap` is chosen to **equalise the
marginal law of `V`**. Hence first-order future-volume information is non-informative *by design*: any
discrimination must come from the **joint** order structure (e.g. interval-cardinality covariance, which is
the discrete proxy for null-congruence focusing / Raychaudhuri expansion `Θ`).

## 4. What is already established (modest)

Matching the `V`-marginal proves insufficiency of **any functional measurable w.r.t. `σ(V)`** (the
σ-algebra generated by the future volumes). This does **not** establish insufficiency of the full local
order, because the observable σ-algebra `σ(C_loc) ⊋ σ(V)`. The discriminating information, if any, lives in
`σ(C_loc) ∖ σ(V)`. One natural channel there (discrete expansion / focusing) has been tried in an ad-hoc
form and was not robust across densities — weak evidence, not a proof, and not exhausting the other joint
channels.

## 5. The precise question

Classify the asymptotic relationship of the two **simple** law sequences `(P_n)`, `(Q_n)` on the unlabelled
suborder into exactly one of four cases:

1. **Asymptotic separation:** `‖P_n − Q_n‖_TV → 1` ⟺ a consistent test exists (both error types → 0) ⟺ an
   intrinsic relabel-invariant trapping observable `T` exists.
2. **Mutual contiguity:** `P_n ◁ Q_n` and `Q_n ◁ P_n` ⟹ no consistent test (asymptotic non-identifiability).
3. **One-sided contiguity:** exactly one of `P_n ◁ Q_n`, `Q_n ◁ P_n` ⟹ still no consistent test, but
   asymmetric (e.g. *absence* of trapping detectable, *presence* not, or vice versa).
4. **Residual complement:** none of the above (`‖P_n−Q_n‖_TV ↛ 1`, neither contiguity). Includes the
   regular sub-regime `0 < liminf ≤ limsup ‖P_n−Q_n‖_TV < 1`, and oscillatory/subsequential behaviour.

(The TV↔consistent-test equivalence is stated for **simple** `P_n, Q_n`. If `H_trap`/`H_no-trap` are later
taken as **composite** families — e.g. all admissible adversarial boundaries — replace it by uniform /
minimax separation between families, `inf/sup` over members.)

## 6. Structural difficulty (what makes this not Erdős–Rényi)

The relation matrix is **not** a product of independent edges: transitivity imposes strong dependence, and
the law is over **unlabelled** isomorphism classes. Sprinkling gives exchangeable points but the induced
order has long-range, geometry-dependent correlations. Standard random-graph contiguity machinery
(likelihood-ratio second-moment, Lindeberg-type control of log-LR) would have to be adapted to a
transitive, manifold-induced dependence structure, and to a functional (interval-cardinality covariance)
rather than edge counts.

## 7. Specific questions for consultation

1. **Sample space / formulation.** What is the right object: the law on isomorphism classes of the *full*
   induced suborder, or of bounded-radius order-neighbourhoods? What does a local order-window preserve?
2. **Adversary power.** Can a flat patch with a shaped future boundary reproduce the *full* suborder law of
   a Schwarzschild-interior sprinkling, or only low-order marginals? Is there a known obstruction?
3. **Contiguity techniques.** Can second-moment / log-likelihood-ratio methods for contiguity of random
   structures be transferred to transitive, dependent (sprinkled-poset) laws? What replaces edge
   independence?
4. **Separating event.** Is there a constructive `A_n` (e.g. an interval-cardinality covariance threshold)
   with `P_n(A_n) → 1`, `Q_n(A_n) → 0`?
5. **Known results.** Are there existing indistinguishability / reconstruction results for random orders
   (or sprinklings) that already settle one of the four cases?
6. **Physical well-posedness.** Are `H_trap` / `H_no-trap` defined without smuggling in a classical
   geometry — i.e. is the discriminating signal genuine focusing, or a boundary/singularity artefact?
